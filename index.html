Operating thesis / 01

Start with the human decision. Build backward to the data, model, and interface.

Research that stops at insight is incomplete. Technology that starts with a model is usually solving the wrong problem. I connect the two.

My work moves through a closed loop: observe reality, encode the requirement, ship the system, measure what changes, and feed the evidence back into the next decision.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Find the real constraint.

    Field research, stakeholder interviews, accessibility studies, and participatory design expose what the system must actually do.

  2. 02

    Encode

    Turn evidence into rules.

    I translate needs into interaction logic, data structures, evaluation criteria, and human-in-the-loop controls.

  3. 03

    Build

    Own the working system.

    Agents, retrieval workflows, data automation, XR interfaces, and physical prototypes move the idea into use.

  4. 04

    Prove

    Make the outcome legible.

    Usability studies, field evaluation, baseline comparison, and independent testing turn performance into decision-grade evidence.
